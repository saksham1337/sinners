# Sinnovation — White-Label Partner Website

A marketing website for Sinnovation, a Bangalore-based team that delivers web, AI, ads, SEO and content work behind the scenes for digital agencies in Canada and the USA.
Agencies resell the work under their own brand. The site turns agency owners into partner leads using real, anonymized proof.

## Who it's for
- **Visitors:** agency owners, founders and operations leads in Canada and the USA looking for a reliable delivery partner. Not small businesses or end clients.
- **Site owner:** the Sinnovation team, who receive and review partner and rate-card enquiries.

## Core features and experience
- **Home:** core promise ("Your brand. Our team. Delivered on time."), the three pillars (Build, Grow, Content), the six white-label guarantees, a short how-it-works, the four engagement models, a case-study teaser built on real numbers, an FAQ teaser and a final call to action.
- **Services:** an overview page plus one page for each of the nine services: website design and development, software and web app development, AI chatbots, AI voice agents, paid ads, SEO, social media management, video editing and clipping, graphic design. Each page covers what's included, how agencies resell it, the delivery flow, turnaround ("Confirmed during onboarding" until real times exist), pricing ("Partner rate card on request") and a short FAQ. AI chatbots, AI voice agents and software do not get invented results.
- **Work:** four anonymized case studies, with no client names, logos or handles:
  - Education (debate, writing and scholar competition coaching): paid ads. Shows 3+ years of continuous work, 1.04M impressions, 103K clicks, 9.91% CTR, 4,880 tracked results, and 12.4M impressions and 7.9M reach on Meta.
  - Consumer tech brand (cameras): social media and influencers. Shows 408% follower growth, a flagship giveaway reaching 172K impressions, and 20+ creators with 78M combined views.
  - Consumer electronics brand across India, Sri Lanka and Bangladesh: the full three-pillar account. Shows Instagram growth of +206%, +394% and +368%, 450+ pieces of content, quarterly reach targets hit, three store websites, and print and digital design. This is the lead case study.
  - Luxury auto dealership group: shown as a **strategy** case study, clearly labelled as planning work. It describes the approach and has no result numbers.
  - **No money figures appear anywhere** (no spend, CPC or CPM). Only reach, clicks, CTR, growth and volume are shown.
- **Partner pricing:** the four engagement models (per project, monthly per client, dedicated team, pilot project), which model suits which service, and a rate-card request form. No prices are shown. All prices come from one central source, set to "on request".
- **How it works:** discovery call → NDA → pilot → shared tools → ongoing delivery → review. Also covers communication, quality commitments and confidentiality basics. Hours and response times use neutral wording until confirmed.
- **About:** who Sinnovation is, why it works only with agencies, the Bangalore-based team, a 150+ websites track record, and experience in consumer tech, education and automotive. A founder section is added once details arrive. Until then it is left out, with no stand-in text.
- **FAQ:** grouped into white-label and confidentiality, working together, quality and revisions, billing and contracts, and services. Pricing answers are honest and contain no numbers.
- **Become a partner form:** name, agency name, website, email, country, services (all 9), engagement models (all 4), approximate monthly volume, message, and an NDA-request checkbox.
- **Rate-card request form:** name, agency, website, email, country, services, volume.
- **Form handling:** both forms check entries before sending, include hidden spam protection, and show clear success and error messages. Each submission is **saved to a private enquiries inbox** and **emailed to contact@sinnovation.si** at the same time.
- **Private enquiries inbox:** a sign-in-protected page where the Sinnovation team views every submission, filters by form type and marks enquiries as handled.
- **Logo:** an original Sinnovation wordmark and symbol, used in the header, the footer, the browser tab icon and the social share image.
- **Supporting pages:** Confidentiality and NDA (plain language), Privacy and Terms (marked as templates pending legal review), and a custom 404 page.
- **Search basics:** a unique title and description for each page aimed at agency search terms (e.g. "white label SEO for agencies"). sinnovation.si is used everywhere as the main domain, with a sitemap and social share tags.

## User flow
1. An agency owner lands on Home, sees the promise and guarantees, and scans the three pillars.
2. They open a service page or the Work page to see what's included and the real proof.
3. They check Partner pricing to choose an engagement model.
4. They submit the Become a partner form (optionally asking for an NDA) or the rate-card request.
5. They see a confirmation message. Sinnovation gets an email and the enquiry appears in the private inbox.
6. A Sinnovation team member signs in, reviews the enquiry and marks it as handled.

## UI/UX feel
Confident, calm and direct, like a capable operations partner rather than a flashy agency. The layout is clean and lets the real numbers lead. Copy uses short sentences, no hype words, no emoji, and US/Canadian spelling. Every headline speaks to the agency ("your clients", "your brand", "your margin"). The word "placeholder" never appears on the site.

**Phase 2 — Real details and visuals**

## Assumptions
- Form submissions are both saved in the private inbox and emailed. The answer picked both options, so both are included.
- contact@sinnovation.si is the site's single contact email. It is shown on the site and receives notification emails, which are sent as "Sinnovation".
- The private inbox uses sign-in with Google or email, and only contact@sinnovation.si can access it. More team emails can be added later.
- All money figures (rupees) are removed from case studies. No converted amounts are shown.
- The auto dealership appears as a labelled strategy case study with no results.
- Case studies launch as clean, number-led layouts without screenshots. Screenshots come in Phase 2.
- The logo is a new original design based on the name and the calm, dependable partner tone. It replaces the generic setup assets.
- Hours, response time, founder, team and legal details are left out or use neutral wording until provided.
- Privacy and Terms are starter templates labelled as pending legal review.
- No client names, competitor names, testimonials, ratings or invented numbers appear anywhere.
